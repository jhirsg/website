Why this matters
Long-term contracts (mortgages, bonds, pensions) are written at a NOMINAL rate. If inflation surprises upward, borrowers benefit (their real debt shrinks); lenders lose. The opposite holds for surprise disinflation. Indexed bonds (TIPS) avoid this risk by paying r + actual π.

The Laffer-curve insight
As π rises, money holders try to ESCAPE money (hold less of it, switch to dollars or hard assets). Eventually the inflation tax base (M/P) shrinks faster than the tax rate (π) rises — total seigniorage FALLS. This is why hyperinflations are self-defeating as a financing strategy.

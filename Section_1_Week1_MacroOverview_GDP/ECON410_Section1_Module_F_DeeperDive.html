Why are they different?

CPI uses the FIXED 2024 basket, so it weights hot dogs and burgers as 200 each. GDP deflator uses the CURRENT 2026 basket (250 hot dogs, 500 burgers). When relative prices change and consumers substitute, CPI misses the substitution and overstates the cost-of-living increase.

Key finding: The MORE consumers substitute toward the cheaper good, the BIGGER the gap between CPI and GDP deflator. CPI (Laspeyres) overstates inflation by this gap. The Boskin Commission (1996) estimated that the U.S. CPI overstates true inflation by about 1.1 percentage points per year because of this substitution bias.

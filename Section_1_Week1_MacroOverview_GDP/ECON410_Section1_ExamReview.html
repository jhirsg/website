What's on the exam?

Questions in this bank come from Mankiw 10/11e Chapter 1–2 (definitions and computation) plus topic-styled practice in the spirit of typical intermediate-macro exam questions. Topic distribution:

  • Measurement basics (~6 Q): recessions, GDP definition, nominal vs. real, stocks vs. flows
  • GDP components (~5 Q): categorize transactions, biggest component, NX, transfers
  • Real/nominal computations (~5 Q): deflator math, CPI math, growth rates
  • Labor market (~5 Q): unemployment rate, LFPR, discouraged workers
  • Production approach (~4 Q): value added, imputed values, what's not in GDP

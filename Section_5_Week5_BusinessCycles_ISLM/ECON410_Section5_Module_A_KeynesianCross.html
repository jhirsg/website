How to read the chart
The 45° line is where Y = E. The blue line is planned expenditure E(Y). They intersect at Y* — the equilibrium output. Above Y*, planned E < Y → firms cut output. Below Y*, planned E > Y → firms expand. Y* is the only stable point.

Why the difference?
A $1 spending increase enters the economy IMMEDIATELY (counts as ΔY in round 1). A $1 tax cut only adds MPC·$1 to round-1 consumption — the rest is saved. So the tax multiplier is smaller in absolute value. The balanced-budget multiplier is exactly 1.

The geometric series in action
Each round: ΔY adds MPC·(prev round). Sum of geometric series = ΔG/(1−MPC). After ~10 rounds, the economy is mostly at the new Y. Real-world: rounds happen continuously — the "multiplier" effect plays out over months/quarters.
